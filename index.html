The Journey to Greatness

From St. Louis to Boston's 18th Championship Banner

Born and raised in St. Louis, Missouri, Jayson Tatum quickly emerged as one of the nation's premier basketball talents at Chaminade College Preparatory School before taking his talents to Duke University for a stellar freshman season.

Drafted third overall by the Boston Celtics in 2017, Tatum made an immediate impact, famously dunking over LeBron James in the Eastern Conference Finals during his rookie playoff run.

"I just try to be the best version of myself every single day and do whatever it takes to win games."

In 2024, Tatum solidified his legacy as a Boston legend by leading the Celtics to their 18th NBA Championship, cementing his place among the league's true elite.

Iconic Career Moments

Explore the games that defined Jayson Tatum's career

May 14, 2023 • Eastern Conference Semifinals

Game 7 Masterpiece vs. Philadelphia 76ers

Tatum set the NBA record for most points scored in a Game 7, dropping 51 points along with 13 rebounds and 5 assists without committing a single turnover in the first three quarters. His relentless scoring blitz dismantled the 76ers and punched Boston's ticket to the ECF.

June 17, 2024 • NBA Finals Game 5

Closing Out Banner 18

In a decisive Game 5 against the Dallas Mavericks, Tatum put up 31 points, 11 rebounds, and 8 assists to secure the Boston Celtics' 18th championship. Overcoming years of playoff heartbreak, he celebrated with his son Deuce on the parquet floor.

April 30, 2021 • Regular Season

60-Point Masterclass vs. San Antonio

Tatum tied Larry Bird's franchise record for most points in a single game, scoring 60 points to rally the Celtics back from a 32-point deficit against the Spurs. Tatum scored 31 of his points in the 4th quarter and overtime.
